Work

What I do, how I do it, and what it produces. Grouped by the kind of work, not the product.

Every partnership follows the same five steps

  1. 01

    Research

    Market and competitor research to find opportunities early.

  2. 02

    First contact

    Tailored email and LinkedIn copy to start the right conversations.

  3. 03

    Relationship

    Proactive communication and value matched to each partner.

  4. 04

    Monitoring

    Trackers and engagement signals flag at-risk accounts.

  5. 05

    Growth

    Spot expansion opportunities and keep follow-ups on schedule.

01

Outreach

Getting the right message to the right person.

Cold and warm outreach to creators, publishers, influencers and partner brands, written for the person receiving it. I research each recipient first, then decide what to offer and how to say it.

  • External email to creators and publishers
  • LinkedIn and social outreach
  • Follow-ups and relationship management

Tools: Expandi, Apollo, Claude, ChatGPT

02

Partnerships

Turning a reply into an agreed deal.

Reciprocal partnerships and exchanges, from first reply to terms agreed. This includes backlink exchanges, listicle inclusions written to match each blog’s own format, and cross-promotion between product makers.

  • Reciprocal deals and backlink exchange
  • Listicle inclusions with reciprocal links
  • Closing deals and ongoing account care

03

Campaigns

Launches and promotions, planned end to end.

Launch outreach, affiliate campaigns, creator video collaborations and promotion planning. I plan who is approached, in what order, with which resources, and keep the timeline moving.

  • Launch outreach and campaign planning
  • Affiliate campaigns and creator collaborations
  • Promo kits and resources for partners

Product lines: WordPress and Shopify

04

Content

Writing that fits where it will be published.

The words around a partnership: hands-on review posts that introduce a product to a partner site’s readers, plus the emails, docs, case studies and outreach copy that move a deal along.

  • Review blogs and listicle entries
  • Emails and outreach copy
  • Docs and case studies

05

Design

Visuals for launches and placements.

Banners and campaign visuals made to the size and style each placement needs, so a partner can publish without extra requests.

  • Campaign banners
  • Social and promotional visuals
  • Assets for partner placements

06

Tracking and reporting

Knowing which relationships need attention.

Trackers for partner status, follow-ups and live placements, with engagement signals that show when an account is at risk, so I can step in before it goes quiet.

  • Partner and placement trackers
  • Monthly reporting on outreach and deals
  • Follow-up scheduling
