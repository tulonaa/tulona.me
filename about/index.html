About

I run outreach, partnerships and campaigns for two software product lines, and I care most about the conversations around the product.

From ad operations to partnerships

I started in December 2021 in digital ad operations at SEBPO, as a campaign manager on Google Ad Manager and FreeWheel for US-based NBCUniversal clients. That job was trafficking, QA across every screen, and delivering to client SLAs.

In 2024 I moved to Get Levrg as a business development executive, running LinkedIn and email outreach with Expandi and Apollo and learning what makes a cold message get a reply.

In May 2025 I joined WPDeveloper as Communication Executive. Today I handle who we write to, what we offer them and how a partnership gets agreed.

How I work

Every partnership gets a structured process: research, first contact, relationship building, health monitoring, then growth and retention. I keep trackers for partner status and follow-ups so at-risk accounts surface early.

I use AI tools daily for research and outreach prep, and I write each message for the person receiving it.
