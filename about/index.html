From ad operations to partnerships

I started in December 2021 in Digital Ad Operations at SEBPO (now renamed Alloyed), as a campaign manager for US-based NBCUniversal clients. That job was trafficking, QA across every screen, reporting and delivering to client SLAs.

In 2024 I moved to Get Levrg as a Business Development Executive, running LinkedIn and email outreach with Expandi and Apollo and learning what makes a cold message get a reply.

In May 2025 I joined WPDeveloper as Communication Executive. Today I manage who we write to, what we offer them and how a partnership gets agreed, across WPDeveloper and Storeware.

How I work

Every partnership gets a structured process: research, first contact, relationship building, health monitoring, then growth and retention. I keep trackers for partner status and follow-ups so at-risk accounts surface early.

I use AI tools daily for research and outreach prep, and I write each message for the person receiving it.

What I’m working on now

Launch outreach and an affiliate campaign for xSpeed Cache, promotion planning for BetterShield, outreach for ThinkRank, and cross-promotion with other Shopify app makers for StoreSEO and StoreFAQ.
