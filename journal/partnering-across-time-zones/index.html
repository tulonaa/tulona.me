Partnering across time zones

The best partnership emails are written for the reader’s morning, not the sender’s.

CollaborationPartnering across time zonesTulona.

Write for their inbox, not yours

When a partner is eight hours away, a reply can take a full day. So each email has to work on its own: who I am, what I’m proposing, and the one thing I need back. No “let’s hop on a call to discuss”.

Send when they’re awake

I schedule outreach to land at the start of the recipient’s working day. It’s a small thing, but an email at the top of the inbox gets a very different reception from one buried under a night of mail.

Make the next step asynchronous

A short question, a draft to react to or a link to a one-page brief lets the other side move at their own pace. Calls are for when there’s already something real to decide.

Respect the calendar

Public holidays, weekends and festival seasons differ everywhere. A quick check before I follow up has saved me from a lot of awkward timing.

Distance is rarely the problem. Unclear emails are.
