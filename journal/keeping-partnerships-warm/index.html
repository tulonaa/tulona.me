Keeping a partnership warm after launch

The work isn’t over when the post goes live.

Global partnershipsEvery follow-up, a reason to reply.Tulona.

Say thanks, specifically

Not “thanks for your support” but “thanks for the section on setup, it’s what readers kept asking about”. It shows I read it.

Share what happened

If a collaboration brought traffic, signups or good conversations, I tell the partner. People remember being kept in the loop.

Check in without an ask

A short note a few weeks later, with something useful and nothing to request, is what makes the next ask feel natural.

Keep a simple record

A tracker of who, what, when and what’s next. It’s not glamorous, but it’s why I can pick a conversation back up months later.

Partnerships don’t end at publish. That’s where they start.
