who is tulona?

I’m Sonia Sultana Tulona, a communications executive in Dhaka. By day I turn good conversations into great collaborations: partnerships, outreach and campaigns for the WPDeveloper and Storeware product lines.

By night, and in the gaps between emails, I read, plan things I may or may not follow through with, and try to work out what my faith asks of an ordinary workday.

These letters are that attempt. They are a student’s notes, not rulings. Please check every reference with someone who knows.
